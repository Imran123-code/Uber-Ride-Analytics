## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze Uber ride data and provide insights into booking activity, revenue generation, cancellations, customer ratings, ride distance, payment methods, vehicle types, and operational demand.

The dashboard is designed to provide an interactive view of ride performance and help identify important business patterns.

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

- **Home** — Project introduction and navigation
- **Overview** — Overall ride and revenue performance
- **Ride & Revenue Overview** — Revenue, customers, vehicle types, and ride distance
- **Cancellation & Customer Insights** — Customer and driver cancellation analysis
- **Operations & Demand Analysis** — Booking patterns and operational demand

## 🔍 Key Analysis

- Total bookings and completed bookings
- Revenue performance
- Cancelled and incomplete rides
- Revenue by vehicle type
- Revenue by payment method
- Customer ratings
- Ride distance trends
- Customer revenue analysis
- Customer cancellation reasons
- Driver cancellation reasons
- Booking demand patterns
